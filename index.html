01

The Four-Move Map

Read first · 3 min

When a company writes about its sustainability work on its own website, the writing usually follows four moves. The moves can appear in one article or across several pages (e.g. About Us, Sustainability Overview, Newsroom).

Each move does a different job — so each move calls for a different tense. Picking the wrong tense weakens the company's credibility. Picking the right one makes the message clear and trustworthy.

Move 01 · Plan

What we aim to do

simple present future

Measurable targets and target year.

→
Move 02 · Results

What we have achieved

present perfect pres. perf. cont.

Achievements with numbers.

→
Move 03 · Actions

What we are doing now

present continuous simple past

Concrete, show-not-tell.

→
Move 04 · Future

What we will do next

future

Follow-up actions and target years.

Tense Reference Card

Simple Present
Describes who the company is

Builds credibility. Used in the PLAN move.

"Our company operates in 30 countries and aims to reach net zero by 2050."
Present Perfect
Shows results so far

Used in the RESULTS move with numbers.

"We have reduced water use by 30 percent since 2020."
Present Perfect Continuous
Ongoing achievement over time

Used in RESULTS with duration.

"We have been investing in renewable energy for ten years."
Present Continuous
Action happening now

Used in PAST/CURRENT ACTIONS.

"Our teams are working with local farmers in East Java."
Simple Past
One completed event in time

Used in PAST/CURRENT ACTIONS for origin stories or specific years.

"In 2018, we launched our first solar plant."
Future
What the company will do next

Used in FUTURE ACTIONS and PLAN.

"By 2030, we will plant two million trees across Indonesia."

04

Website Writing Assignment

Free-write · four short paragraphs

Write a short sustainability story for the website of PT Hijau Nusantara.

Use the brief below to write ONE short paragraph for each of the four moves. Use the right tense in each paragraph.

▸ Company brief — PT Hijau Nusantara (beverage company)
  • Target (Plan): 100% recyclable packaging by 2030
  • Results so far: 65% of packaging is recyclable; 40 million plastic bottles collected since 2021
  • Current action: Partnering with 200 waste banks in Java; running consumer return programs
  • Next step (Future): Expand to Sumatra and Sulawesi by 2027; launch refill stations in 50 cities
Move 01 · Plan

What we aim to do

Use simple present and/or future. State the target and the year.
Move 02 · Results

What we have achieved

Use present perfect. Include the numbers from the brief.
Move 03 · Past / Current Actions

What we are doing right now

Use present continuous (and past where helpful). Show, don't tell.
Move 04 · Future Actions

What we will do next

Use future. Mention the next regions and the future year.
